# Privacy Policy for HerCycle Health

**Effective Date:** October 9, 2026

## Introduction
HerCycle Health ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application (the "App").

## Information We Collect
The HerCycle Health App is designed to prioritize your privacy. 
- **Health and Medical Data:** You may voluntarily input health and medical information (e.g., menstrual cycle data, blood pressure, or general symptoms) to receive assessment insights.
- **Local Storage:** All sensitive health and medical data entered into the App is stored **locally on your device**. 
- **Anonymous Telemetry:** The App may collect anonymous usage data (telemetry) to improve the assessment flow. **All medical values are stripped and anonymized** before being processed for telemetry. We do not collect or transmit personally identifiable information (PII) or raw medical values to our servers.

## How We Use Your Information
- To provide and calculate health assessment results locally on your device.
- To improve the App’s performance through anonymous, non-identifiable usage metrics.

## Disclosure of Your Information
We **do not** sell, trade, rent, or share your personal or health information with third parties. Since your sensitive data remains on your device, we cannot provide it to third parties.

## Data Security
Your health data is securely saved offline using your device's native storage mechanisms. We strongly encourage you to secure your device with a passcode, Face ID, or Touch ID to protect your locally stored data.

## Children's Privacy
Our App does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Effective Date."

## Contact Us
If you have questions or comments about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at support@hercycle.appray.ai.
